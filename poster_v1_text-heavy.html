KopiKaki

A voice-first AI companion that helps older caregivers in Singapore complete online support forms, on their own terms.

1The problem: support exists, but can't be reached

62median age of daily caregivers [1]
Unawaremany don't know support is online [13]
Vision + cluttera key barrier to digital use [9, 11]
Won't askhelp is avoided, loneliness follows [3, 5, 6]
Seniors Go Digital teaches general skills only. Nothing is built for caregiving tasks.  [14]
Older caregivers with declining vision give up on online support forms, and never ask for help.

3Three lenses, one design

STEM

Speech and screen-reading AI, built for low vision and low confidence. [9, 11]

Governance & Business

Works with agencies, not around them. Caregiving already costs time and work. [14–18]

Society & Humanities

Dignity first: do it yourself, without being a burden. [3, 5, 6]

Social need sets the goal. Governance sets the limits. STEM sets the interface.
