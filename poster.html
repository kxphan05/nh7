2The solution: Kopi reads, explains, fills in. You decide.

Working prototype. Listens first (ELIZA-style), then guides the form one question at a time, lights up the matching box and reads every entry back.

What it is & who it is for

A chat companion beside an agency form, for older caregivers whose eyesight makes dense forms hard to read. Offered by community partners, never forced [3, 6, 9, 10]. Speaks English, Mandarin, Malay and Tamil.

What the AI does

  • Reads and explains each section in plain words
  • Listens to typed or spoken answers
  • Fills the non-sensitive boxes, reads each back
  • Spots scam wording

What the AI never does

  • Types NRIC, income or bank details
  • Presses Submit for you
  • Predicts how much you will get
  • Guesses when unsure: it hands over

Where the lenses meet: each design rule comes from two of them
Society × GovernanceDignity + consent: the user types private details and presses Submit. Kopi never acts alone.
STEM × SocietyLow vision + not wanting to ask: voice-first, one lit-up box at a time, offered but never forced.
Governance × STEMAgency data rules: Kopi sees only the form, keeps nothing, and Singpass stays with the user [14].

5Stakeholder concerns

FeasibilityNo agency system changes: Kopi sits beside the public form. Prototype built; the pilot tests it.
CostNot yet costed, and we say so. The pilot records staff time and cost per completed form.
Ethics & privacyOpt-in, on-device, deleted after use; the user always has the final say. We test across languages and ages for bias.
ImplementationFree setup by hospitals, CCs and volunteers [9, 10]. If Kopi is wrong, it says "not sure".

6Why someone should act

CaregiversFinish it themselves, with dignity. Ask: try it.
AgenciesMore schemes reach the people they are for [13, 14]. Ask: let Kopi sit beside your form.
CCs & hospitalsA tool for outreach they already do [9, 10]. Ask: host the set-up.
