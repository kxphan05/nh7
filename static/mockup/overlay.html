Home Caregiving Grant (HCG)

Monthly cash payouts to help families caring for persons with moderate to severe disabilities.

Who can get it

The care recipient must be a Singapore Citizen, or a PR with a parent, child or spouse who is a living Singapore Citizen. They must live in Singapore, and not in a residential long-term care institution.

Care needs

The care recipient must permanently require assistance with at least 3 of the 6 activities of daily living, confirmed by a disability assessment.

Means test

Household monthly income per person of $4,800 or less.

Payout

Household monthly income per personMonthly payout
$0 – $1,500$600
$1,501 – $3,600$400
$3,601 – $4,800$200

How to apply

Online (recommended): through AIC's eFASS with Singpass. Shorter approval wait.
Hard copy: email apply@aic.sg or visit an AIC Link. Takes longer to process.

Processing

Processing may take up to four weeks. You will be notified by mail.

⚠️ Scam warning

AIC staff will never ask you to transfer money or disclose bank log-in details over a phone call. If in doubt, call the ScamShield Helpline at 1799.
